Unscripted Worlds Uploader

The small program Victor Bastos uses to upload original videos to the Unscripted Worlds YouTube channel.

What Unscripted Worlds makes: short videos about a tiny simulated civilization, a village of simple computer-controlled characters who farm, trade and try to survive, and the experiments run on it. Every video is produced by the operator from original simulation data.

What the uploader does

  • Runs only on the operator's own computer. There is no website login, no server and no other users.
  • When the operator asks it to, it uploads one finished video to the Unscripted Worlds channel with its title, description, tags and privacy setting.
  • Before uploading, it reads the channel's name to confirm it is connected to the right account.

Google and YouTube data

The uploader signs in with Google OAuth and asks for two YouTube permissions: youtube.upload (upload videos) and youtube.readonly (read the channel's name). It does not read comments, subscribers, analytics or any other account data, and it never shares Google user data with anyone. Details are in the Privacy Policy. This tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service.
